YEAR 7 / SENIOR SCHOOL

Rugby School Dubai — App Directory & Links

A central guide for parents and students to track all required platforms, web portals, mobile apps, and access methods for 2026/27.

⏰ Morning Registration Reminder: Senior School drop-off is from 7:40 AM. Students must be in their form rooms by 7:50 AM for registration and the National Anthem.
About This Directory:

This page details every app required for Year 7 at Rugby School Dubai. Each tool is broken down into Student Access, Parent Access, Main Point of Contact, and Other Info.

  • 📘 Official Reference: View the full Rugby School Dubai Parent Handbook.
  • Use the search bar below to filter apps by name, feature (e.g., homework, canteen, photos, absence, fees), or contact. Tap card titles on mobile to collapse or expand.
iSAMS / iParent
Main school communication platform for viewing timetables, academic reports, attendance, rewards, school calendar, and contact updates.
🎓 Student Access
🌐 Web Portal: rugbyschool.students.isams.cloud
📱 Mobile App: iStudent App (iOS / Android)
👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Access
🌐 Web Portal: rugbyschool.parents.isams.cloud
📱 Mobile App: iParent App (iOS / Android)
📞 Main Point of Contact Parent Relations Manager: prm@rugbyschooldubai.ae
Admissions Team: admissions@rugbyschooldubai.ae
ℹ️ Other Info School App Code: RSD1. Registration credentials are emailed by Admissions. Year 7 operates on an alternating Week A / Week B schedule. To print a clean PDF timetable, log into the parent web portal and go to Academic Information ➔ School Timetable.
Sparx Maths
Online personalized mathematics learning and homework platform used across Year 7, tailored to individual student progress.
🎓 Student Access
🌐 Web Login: auth.sparxmaths.uk/student
👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Access
🌐 Parent Portal: Direct access link emailed in weekly homework updates.
📖 Parent Guide: sparxmaths.com/parents
📞 Main Point of Contact Child's Year 7 Maths Teacher or Form Tutor
ℹ️ Other Info Student accounts are created automatically by the school. Teachers select weekly topics covered in class, and Sparx generates personalized question sets with video support.
SOCS
Management platform for Co-Curricular Activities (CCAs), preferential activity choices, sports fixtures, and team schedules for Senior School.
🎓 Student Access
🌐 Web Portal: socscms.com/login/student
👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Access
🌐 Web Portal: socscms.com/login/parent
(Search "Rugby School Dubai". Can be saved as a Web App to mobile home screens.)
📞 Main Point of Contact Senior School (Y7–Y13): lreiners@rugbyschooldubai.ae
ℹ️ Other Info Parent accounts are activated using the registered iSAMS email address. Confirmed CCAs can be viewed under Activities ➔ My Activities or on the integrated calendar. Afternoon CCAs finish at 4:30 PM. For late registrations, contact Senior Reception.

Spare App / Food Nation
Cashless payment system for purchasing food items in the school canteen and Senior School dining facilities.
🎓 Student Access
💳 Physical Spare Card / Digital ID on-site.
(Note: Phones and smartwatches are not permitted for payments on-site for Senior School students.)
👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Access
🌐 Official Site: paywithspare.com
📱 Mobile App: Spare (iOS / Android)
📞 Main Point of Contact Catering Feedback: info@foodnationme.com
Parent Relations Manager: prm@rugbyschooldubai.ae
ℹ️ Other Info Be sure to update your child's profile to Year 7 in the Spare app settings so funds transfer correctly. The standard AED 26 hot meal package includes soup, salad, main course, and dessert.
